# Code of Conduct

DropSync welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

## Our expectations

Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique ideas and code constructively. Assume good intent, accept corrections, and respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries. Help newcomers participate.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, spam and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to pressure people into sharing credentials, private files or personal contact details.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository owner, [Taxperia](https://github.com/Taxperia), through a private contact method they publish on their profile. If none is available, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without naming affected people or including confidential details. Security vulnerabilities belong in [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Taxperia/dropsync/security/advisories/new).

Maintainers will review reports with care and limit disclosure to what is needed to address them. Depending on the circumstances, they may request an edit, remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban repeated or serious violations. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and protect the people affected.
